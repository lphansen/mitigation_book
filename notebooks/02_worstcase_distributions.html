2.2. Distorted density for climate models #

The decision-maker has a baseline probability \(dP_t(\theta)\) that weights each of the 144 climate models equally at every time instant, \(t\), and makes an adjustment for ambiguity by solving Problem 3.2 of the paper:

\[ \min_{Q_t \ge 0,\; \int_\Theta Q_t(\theta)\,dP_t(\theta)=1}\; \frac{\partial V}{\partial y}(X_t)\,\mathcal{E}_t \int_\Theta \theta\,Q_t(\theta)\,dP_t(\theta) \;+\; \xi_a \int_\Theta Q_t(\theta)\,\log Q_t(\theta)\,dP_t(\theta) \]

with the minimizing relative density given by an exponential tilt:

\[ Q_t^{\ast}(\theta) \;\propto\; \exp\!\Bigl[-\frac{1}{\xi_a}\,\frac{\partial V}{\partial y}(X_t)\,\mathcal{E}_t\,\theta\Bigr]. \]

2.3. Distorted density for the time of the first jump #

By conditioning first on the state-variable history, we find that the probability that a jump has occurred by time \(t\) under the uncertainty-adjusted probabilities is

\[ F_t \;=\; 1 - \exp\!\Bigl[ - \int_{0}^{t} \sum_{\ell=1}^{L} G^{*\ell}_\tau\,\mathcal J^{\ell}\!\bigl(X_\tau\bigr)\, d\tau \Bigr], \]

which increases in \(t\). The implied density obtained by differentiating with respect to \(t\) is

\[ f_t \;=\; \sum_{\ell=1}^{L} G^{*\ell}_t\,\mathcal J^{\ell}(X_t)\, \exp\!\Bigl[ - \int_{0}^{t} \sum_{\hat\ell=1}^{L} G^{*\hat\ell}_\tau\,\mathcal J^{\hat\ell}\!\bigl(X_\tau\bigr)\, d\tau \Bigr]. \]

We unpack this density by jump type,

\[ f_t \;=\; \sum_{\ell=1}^{L} f_t^{\ell}, \]

where

\[ f_t^{\ell} \overset{\mathrm{def}}{=} G^{*\ell}_t\,\mathcal J^{\ell}(X_t)\, \exp\!\Bigl[ - \int_{0}^{t} \sum_{\hat\ell=1}^{L} G^{*\hat\ell}_\tau\,\mathcal J^{\hat\ell}\!\bigl(X_\tau\bigr)\, d\tau \Bigr], \qquad \ell = 1,\dots,L. \]
